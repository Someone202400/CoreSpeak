Why CoreSpeak

Designed for Communication

🧩

12 Semantic Categories

From Common words to Emergency phrases — each category is color-coded using the Fitzgerald Key system for intuitive navigation.

🔊

Real-Time Speech

Every tap speaks the word instantly. Build full sentences and hear them read aloud with natural browser-based speech synthesis.

🚨

Emergency Mode

A dedicated Emergency category with a pulsing alarm button that triggers a repeating vocal alert — critical for urgent situations.

☁️

Cloud Sync

Sign in with Google to save your sentence history. Access it from any device, anywhere in the world.

🔄

Backspace & Clear

Mistake? Use the Backspace button to remove the last word or Clear All to start fresh. Full control over your sentence.

📋

Phrase History

Every spoken sentence is logged with a timestamp. Replay past phrases from the History sidebar with one click.
